# ASO Keyword Observations

Research date: 2026-10-20.

## Method and strict limitations
- The App Store and Google Play **in-store search could not be accessed**: fetches to apps.apple.com and play.google.com were blocked or failed DNS.
- The observations below come from **web search results restricted to apps.apple.com / play.google.com** for each phrase. That is a proxy for title/subtitle keyword relevance. It is **not** in-store rank.
- **No search volumes, difficulty scores or install estimates are given. None were observed.**
- The order below is the order the web search returned. It shows which listings are indexed for the phrase, not App Store ranking.
- Next step (manual): check real in-store ranks on a US device or with an ASO tool (AppTweak, Sensor Tower, AppFigures) before finalizing metadata.

- **Observation:** BIGVU uses "Teleprompter & Captions" as its Play title. Many small apps use the "Teleprompter + Captions" title pattern. Incumbents are present.

## Cross-cutting observations
1. **Generic-name saturation.** Dozens of listings are titled "Teleprompter …". Exact-match titles look like the dominant ASO tactic (inference from titles seen).
2. **Name conflict.** "Promptly Pro Teleprompter" (id6757360688) and "Prompty: AI Video Teleprompter" (id6767272173) exist, and a non-teleprompter "Promptly" (id6797143717) is also listed. A "Promptly" brand would compete for its own name. **Recommend a naming and trademark review (founder decision).**
3. **Uncontested-looking modifiers.** No incumbent was seen for combinations like "voice follow", "hook", "script to captions", or "Shorts teleprompter". These are candidates for subtitle and keyword-field testing (UNVERIFIED for volume).
4. **Platform trademarks.** Competitors avoid "TikTok"/"Instagram" in titles and use "Reels" and "Shorts" (generic words) instead.

## Candidate metadata to test (hypotheses, not recommendations of volume)
- Title (30 chars): "[Brand]: AI Teleprompter" or "[Brand] Teleprompter & Captions".
- Subtitle (30 chars): "Voice-Follow Script, Captions" or "Voice Teleprompter for Reels".
- iOS keyword field ideas: voice,follow,tracking,scroll,captions,subtitles,srt,script,hook,reels,shorts,creator,autocue,prompter,ai,writer.
- Play: put "voice tracking teleprompter with captions for Reels and Shorts" naturally in the short description.
