# Privacy policy — draft (requires legal review before publication)

Last updated: [date]

**Summary:** Promptly works without an account. Scripts, recordings, captions and settings are stored
on your device. We do not upload your videos.

1. **Data stored on your device:** scripts and their versions, recordings, captions, settings, usage
   counters (e.g. free Voice Follow takes). Delete everything in Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all.
2. **Camera and microphone:** used only while you record. Recordings are saved on your device.
3. **Speech recognition:** Voice Follow and captions use Apple's or Google's speech recognition. Promptly
   requests on-device processing where available; on some devices or languages the platform provider may
   process audio on its servers under its own privacy policy.
4. **AI Script Studio (optional):** when you tap an AI action, the text you entered is sent to our server
   and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a draft. We do not store the text after processing and
   do not use it to train models. [Confirm provider retention terms.]
5. **Purchases:** handled by Apple / Google; RevenueCat processes purchase records and an anonymous app
   user ID to manage your subscription.
6. **Analytics:** [provider TBD] anonymous usage events (e.g. "recording completed") without content or
   personal data; can be turned off in Settings.
7. **Children:** not directed to children under 13.
8. **Contact:** [support email].
