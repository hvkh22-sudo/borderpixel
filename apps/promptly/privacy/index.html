Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short

  • No account is needed. Your scripts, recordings, captions and settings are stored on your device.
  • We never upload your videos.
  • AI Script Studio is optional and asks for your consent before sending any text.
  • We do not sell your data and do not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Scripts and their earlier versions, recordings, captions, app settings and simple usage counters (for example, how many free Voice Follow takes you have used) are stored only on your device. You can delete everything in Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes this data.

Camera and microphone

Promptly uses the camera and microphone only while you record. Recordings are saved on your device. They are shared only when you choose to export or share them.

Speech recognition

Voice Follow and automatic captions use the speech recognition built into your device (Apple Speech on iOS, Android speech recognition on Android). Promptly requests on-device processing where it is available. On some devices or languages, Apple or Google may process audio on their servers under their own privacy policies.

AI Script Studio (optional)

Before you use AI Script Studio for the first time, Promptly asks for your permission. When you then tap an AI action, the text you entered (for example a topic or a script) is sent to our server, hosted on Cloudflare, and to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), which generates a draft. Our server does not store this text. Anthropic does not use it to train models and may retain it for up to 30 days for trust and safety purposes before deleting it.

To apply fair-use limits, our server keeps request counters linked to the anonymous purchase identifier described below, and short-lived daily counters per IP address. These counters contain no text.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to manage them. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous app identifier and your purchase history so that your plan works and can be restored. We do not receive your payment details.

Usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, Promptly records anonymous events such as “recording completed”. These never include your scripts, transcripts, audio, video or personal details.

Children

Promptly is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can delete all on-device data at any time from Settings. To ask about the anonymous purchase identifier or usage counters held by us or our providers, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest version above.
