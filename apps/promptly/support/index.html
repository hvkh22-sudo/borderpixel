Is the script visible in my video?

No. The script is shown on your screen only. The camera records the scene without it.

Voice Follow lost my place

Tap the line you are reading to jump there, or read a few words from the script so it can find you again. Voice Follow needs iOS speech recognition or Android 13 or newer.

Where are my recordings?

In Takes, inside Promptly. Use Export to save them to Photos or share them.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription in the app, or your App Store / Google Play account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Promptly Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data removes all scripts, takes and settings from the device.
