Privacy Policy

BorderPixel: Passport Photo · Last updated 7 August 2026

Plain-English summary

  • BorderPixel uses the camera or a photo you select only to evaluate and format a passport photo.
  • The working photo and facial measurements are processed on your device. BorderPixel does not operate a photo-processing server.
  • We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or use your photo to train AI.
  • A finished image leaves the app only when you deliberately choose an iOS Save or Share destination.
  • Apple handles the one-time in-app purchase; we do not receive your payment-card details.

1. Information handled by the app

Photos and camera input. Photos you take or select are used to detect a face, evaluate visible photo requirements, assist cropping, and create an export. This processing occurs on your device. BorderPixel does not transmit the working photo to us.

Face measurements. Apple’s on-device Vision framework produces temporary measurements such as face position, eye visibility, and head angle. They are used for the active session, not to identify you, and are not sent to us.

Purchases. Apple processes the one-time in-app purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide transaction status needed to unlock or restore the purchase.

Support messages. If you contact support, we receive the address and information you choose to include. Please do not attach a passport photo or identity document.

2. How information is used

The app uses the working photo and temporary measurements to capture, evaluate, crop, resize, straighten where supported, preview, and export the photo. It does not use them for advertising, profiling, identity recognition, or AI training.

Support information is used to answer the request, diagnose the described issue, protect the service from abuse, and maintain necessary support records.

3. Storage and retention

Working images and measurements are held in memory for the active editing session. The app clears them on terminal actions such as Done, Retake, or Restart, on supported back-navigation cleanup, when iOS terminates the app, and during inactive memory-pressure cleanup. A backgrounded session may remain in memory so you can resume; the app places an opaque cover over its own inactive screen.

BorderPixel does not create an account or store your working photo on our servers. Copies you deliberately save or share remain at the iOS destination you selected.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain necessary support records, protect legal rights, or meet legal obligations, and is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

4. Save, Share, Photos, and iCloud

When you choose Save or Share, iOS passes the finished image to the destination you select, such as Photos, Files, email, messaging, or cloud storage. The system interface may show a preview. Your chosen destination may store, sync, or transmit the image under its own terms and settings. If iCloud Photos is enabled, a copy saved to Photos may sync through Apple’s service.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Apple may make diagnostics available to developers according to your device and App Store settings; BorderPixel does not add photo pixels or face measurements to app logs.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not share working photos or face measurements with advertisers, data brokers, or AI-training providers. Apple handles App Store purchases, and your chosen Save or Share destination handles the image only when you invoke it.

7. Security

The product minimizes exposure by keeping working-photo processing on-device, avoiding accounts and a photo backend, limiting accepted input size and type, clearing sensitive session state on supported terminal paths, and covering BorderPixel-owned inactive screens. No security measure can eliminate every risk on a device or in a destination you choose.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children. A parent or guardian may operate it to prepare a dependent’s photo. Do not allow a child to send support messages or personal documents without appropriate adult supervision.

9. Your choices and rights

You can stop a session, deny camera access, use the system photo picker instead, or delete saved copies from the destination where you placed them. Because we do not maintain an account or server copy of working photos, we generally have no such photo to retrieve or delete.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning support correspondence. Send a request to hvkh22@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and fulfill the request, but you should not send a passport photo or identity document unless legally necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected before the affected app version is released.
