Passport photo guidance, without the guesswork

Check before you submit.

BorderPixel helps you capture, review, crop, and export a US passport photo while keeping the working image on your iPhone.

  • On-device processing
  • No account
  • No subscription
  • No AI edits

BorderPixel is an independent helper, not a government service. It cannot guarantee that an agency will accept a photo.

What it checks

Clear results. Honest limits.

The app separates checks it can perform from items you still need to confirm. It does not turn uncertainty into a false “approved” result.

  • ✓Photo size and framing
  • ✓Head position and eye visibility
  • ✓Sharpness and background guidance
  • ✓Assisted crop for the final file

Product principles

Your photo stays in the app’s local session.

BorderPixel does not operate a photo-processing server. A finished image leaves the app only when you choose an iOS Save or Share destination.

One export unlock. No recurring plan.

Checking and previewing are free. A single non-consumable App Store purchase unlocks the clean export and can be restored through Apple.
